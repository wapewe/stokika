1. Regulatory Compliance

stokika is committed to operating in full compliance with all applicable laws and regulations in Kenya and across Africa.

2. Kenyan Regulatory Framework

2.1 Data Protection Act, 2019

  • Registered with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner
  • Compliance with data processing principles
  • Protection of personal and business data
  • Regular data protection audits

2.2 Companies Act

  • Registered business entity in Kenya
  • Compliance with corporate governance standards
  • Annual financial reporting
  • Proper business licensing

2.3 Tax Compliance

  • KRA PIN registration and compliance
  • VAT registration and remittance
  • PAYE for employees
  • Corporate tax obligations

3. Financial Services Compliance

3.1 Anti-Money Laundering (AML)

  • Customer due diligence procedures
  • Transaction monitoring systems
  • Suspicious activity reporting
  • Record keeping requirements

3.2 Know Your Customer (KYC)

  • Identity verification for all users

  • Enhanced due diligence for high-risk customers
