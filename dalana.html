About stokika

Empowering Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises across Kenya and Africa with smart stock access, supplier networks, and business growth tools.

Our Story

stokika was born from a simple observation: countless talented entrepreneurs across Kenya and Africa have the skills, determination, and market demand to succeed, but lack access to inventory. We set out to change that by creating a platform that bridges the gap between ambition and achievement, making stock financing accessible, transparent, and tailored to the unique rhythms of small businesses.

Our Promise

We promise to stand by every vendor, supplier, and entrepreneur who trusts us with their business growth. With transparent pricing, flexible repayment cycles, and dedicated support, we're committed to being more than a financing platform—we're your growth partner, invested in your success every step of the way.

Mission

To empower micro, small and medium businesses across Africa by providing accessible stock financing and tools of trade, enabling sustainable growth without cash flow constraints.

Vision

A future where no business dream stalls due to lack of stock. Every vendor, from mama mboga to service providers, has the inventory they need to thrive.

Mission Statement

We exist to democratize access to business inventory and tools across Africa, creating pathways for micro, small and medium enterprises to scale sustainably through smart financing, verified supplier networks, and business intelligence tools.
