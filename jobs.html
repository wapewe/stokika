Field Agent

Multiple Positions

Location: Various counties in Kenya

Reports To: Supervisor

Role Summary

A Field Agent is responsible for acquiring, onboarding and supporting micro, small and medium businesses that requires stock advances and or suppliers, manufacturers and distributors. They collect accurate business data, verify vendors, support deliveries, track repayments, and act as stokika's representative in the field.

Key Responsibilities

  • Identify and recruit vendors
  • Publicizing and creating awareness
  • Verify vendor businesses (photos, GPS, documents)
  • Collect and submit accurate data
  • Coordinate stock deliveries, stock evaluation & supervision
  • Support repayment follow-up
  • Report fraud signs
  • Maintain accounts and logistics

Minimum Requirements

  • Basic smartphone & web navigation skills
  • Fluent in local languages
  • Experience in sales, fieldwork, marketing, businness management, community outreach or any other related field is an added advantage
