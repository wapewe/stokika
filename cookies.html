1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website. They help us provide a better user experience and understand how our platform is used.

3. Third-Party Cookies

We use services from third parties that may set cookies:

  • Google Analytics: Website analytics
  • Supabase: Database and authentication
  • Facebook Pixel: Marketing and analytics
  • Payment Processors: Secure transactions

4. How to Control Cookies

4.1 Browser Settings

Most browsers allow you to:

  • View and delete cookies
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Block all cookies
  • Clear cookies when closing browser

4.2 Browser-Specific Instructions

  • Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies
  • Firefox: Options > Privacy & Security > Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Cookies
  • Edge: Settings > Privacy > Cookies

4.3 Opt-Out Tools

  • Google Analytics Opt-out: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout
  • Network Advertising Initiative: optout.networkadvertising.org

5. Impact of Disabling Cookies

Disabling cookies may affect platform functionality:

  • Unable to stay logged in
  • Loss of personalized settings
  • Reduced platform performance
  • Some features may not work

6. Local Storage

We also use browser local storage for:

  • Offline functionality
  • Form data persistence
  • User preferences
  • Referral tracking
