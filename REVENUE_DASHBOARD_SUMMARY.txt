                           WHAT WAS BUILT
================================================================================

A comprehensive, real-time revenue dashboard for the Stokika admin panel that:

✅ Tracks revenue from 3 sources:
   • Field Agent kit payments (KES 2,500 each)
   • Supervisor kit payments (KES 3,500 each)
   • Vendor order commissions (service fees)

✅ Tracks expenses:
   • Supplier payments (money out)

✅ Calculates key metrics:
   • Total Revenue
   • Net Profit
   • Profit Margin %

✅ Provides time-based filtering:
   • Today
   • This Week
   • This Month

✅ Auto-refreshes every 60 seconds

✅ Shows detailed breakdown tables

✅ Fully responsive (desktop, tablet, mobile)

================================================================================
                       REVENUE CALCULATIONS
================================================================================

Total Revenue = Field Agents Revenue + Supervisors Revenue + Vendors Revenue

Net Profit = Total Revenue - Supplier Payments

Profit Margin = (Net Profit / Total Revenue) × 100

Field Agents Revenue = SUM(kit_amount) WHERE user_type='field_agent' 
                       AND payment_status IN ('verified','completed')

Supervisors Revenue = SUM(kit_amount) WHERE user_type='supervisor' 
                      AND payment_status IN ('verified','completed')

Vendors Revenue = SUM(service_fee_amount) WHERE vendor_id IS NOT NULL 
                  AND status IN ('completed','delivered')

Supplier Payments = SUM(amount) WHERE status IN ('paid','completed')
