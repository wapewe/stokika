1. Employment Agreement

By submitting your application and accepting employment with stokika, you agree to the following terms and conditions.

2. Employment Status

Field Agents and Supervisors are independent contractors working on a commission or performance-based compensation structure.

3. Duties and Responsibilities

3.1 Field Agents

  • Recruit and onboard eligible vendors
  • Verify vendor identity and business legitimacy
  • Collect accurate business data
  • Support vendors during stock advance cycles
  • Track and report repayments
  • Maintain field presence and vendor relationships

3.2 Supervisors

  • Oversee field agent teams
  • Verify vendor applications and data accuracy
  • Approve or reject stock advance requests
  • Conduct fraud prevention audits
  • Train and coach field agents
  • Submit weekly operational reports

4. Code of Conduct

  • Maintain professional behavior at all times
  • Represent stokika with integrity
  • Protect confidential information
  • Avoid conflicts of interest
  • Report any fraudulent activity immediately
  • Comply with all company policies

5. Compensation

Compensation is based on performance metrics including vendor acquisition, stock approvals, and repayment rates. Payment terms will be communicated separately.

6. Confidentiality

You agree to maintain strict confidentiality of all vendor data, business information, and company operations. This obligation continues after employment termination.

7. Data Protection

You must handle all personal and business data in compliance with Kenya's Data Protection Act and stokika's privacy policies.

8. Fraud Prevention

  • Zero tolerance for fraudulent activity
  • Immediate termination for fraud or collusion
  • Legal action may be pursued
  • Obligation to report suspicious activities

9. Equipment and Tools

Field Agents must provide their own smartphone with internet access. stokika may provide additional tools or equipment as needed.

10. Training and Development

You agree to participate in all required training sessions and maintain knowledge of stokika's products, services, and procedures.

11. Performance Evaluation

Performance will be evaluated regularly based on key metrics. Continued employment depends on meeting performance standards.

12. Termination

12.1 By Employee

You may resign with 2 weeks written notice.

12.2 By stokika

stokika may terminate employment for:

  • Poor performance
  • Violation of terms or policies
  • Fraudulent activity
  • Misconduct
  • Business restructuring

13. Liability and Indemnification

You agree to indemnify stokika against any losses arising from your negligence, fraud, or violation of these terms.

14. Non-Compete

During employment and for 6 months after, you agree not to work for direct competitors or solicit stokika vendors for competing services.

15. Dispute Resolution

Any disputes will be resolved through mediation. If unresolved, disputes will be handled in Kenyan courts under Kenyan law.

16. Amendments

stokika reserves the right to modify these terms. You will be notified of significant changes.

17. Acceptance

By checking the agreement box and submitting your application, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to these terms and conditions.
